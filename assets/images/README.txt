Optional images go in this folder. None are required; the site looks finished without them.

  profile.jpg    your portrait            -> About section
  college.jpg    University of Georgia    -> Education section
  project-1.jpg  soil health probe        -> Projects (first project)
  project-2.jpg  anesthesia system demo   -> Projects (second project)

After adding a file, open index.html, search for "OPTIONAL", and delete the two
comment lines around the matching <figure> so it appears on the page.
PNG or WebP also work: just change the file extension in that <img src="...">.
