Projects

Soil Health Monitoring Probe

Innovation Academy, Alpharetta, GA | Dec 2024 to May 2026

A field probe that measures soil conditions and reports soil health to farm owners in real time.

  1. DHT11, NPK, pH sensors
  2. ESP8266
  3. Wi-Fi dashboard
  4. Soil disease risk flag
  • Designed the sensor circuit and an Autodesk Fusion enclosure around an ESP8266, integrating a DHT11 (temperature and humidity), an NPK sensor (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium), and a pH sensor.
  • Programmed the ESP8266 in C++ (Arduino) to sample the sensors and transmit readings over Wi-Fi to a live dashboard, where an AI detection model flags soil disease risk.
  • Field-tested across 2 acres for 12 weeks, then iterated: ESP8266 deep-sleep mode to cut power draw, a more durable enclosure, and additional soil metrics, with further refinements identified for future versions.

Automated Anesthesia System Demonstration

Innovation Academy, Alpharetta, GA | Aug 2025 to May 2026

A prototype that calculates anesthesia dosage from live patient vitals.

  1. Live vitals
  2. Dosage calculation
  3. Dosage display
  • Designed a prototype that read real-time heart rate, blood pressure, ECG, and body temperature, combining live vitals with prior patient information to calculate and display dosage levels.
  • Built a demonstration unit that shows how the calculated dosage adjusts as patient vitals change.
  • Presented the working project at the school's engineering showcase to local employers.
